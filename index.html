A visual guide to change in daily activity

Reshaping the Day

When older adults move less over the years, the change is rarely even. Activity can fade at certain hours, start later, or drift across the day. This demo shows how to see those changes and why they matter.

1 · Change has two parts

Same total, different day

Person A and Person B each moved about 5% less at their second visit. A daily total records the same change twice. Their days tell two different stories.

  • Timing: earlier or later
  • Amount: more or less

Gray track: visit 1. Dark bar: visit 2. A total cannot tell these two people apart.

Every push has two parts. Sideways is timing. Up or down is amount. The method estimates both at every minute of the day, so a later morning and a quieter evening stay distinct.

2 · Cohorts share patterns

A few patterns describe most change

Look across hundreds of women and individual changes stop looking random. On average, activity fell across the day. Activity before mid-morning drifted a little later, and the rest of the day drifted a little earlier.

Beyond the average, four patterns capture most of how people differ. Each person's change is a mix of them.
