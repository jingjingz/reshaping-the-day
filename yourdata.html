Try your own data

Upload two visits of minute-level activity counts from the same person. The page builds each visit's average day, fits the pushes between them and shows how the day changed. Your files stay on your computer: nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Your files

Choose two files, or use a sample person.

What files work

  • A CSV with a timestamp column, such as 2024-03-01 06:00:00 or 3/1/2024 6:00:00 AM.
  • Either one counts column (vector magnitude, named counts or vm) or three axis columns named axis1, axis2, axis3.
  • Epochs of one minute or shorter. Shorter epochs are summed to minutes, axis by axis, before the vector magnitude.
  • About a week of wear at each visit. Days need at least 14 hours of wear between 6 am and midnight, and four or more valid days give a stable average day.
  • Hip-worn ActiGraph counts match the demo best. Other devices work, scaled to the demo's range.

Sample files: visit1.csv and visit2.csv (synthetic).

Your mix of the four patterns

How much of the change the shared patterns account for.

Open this mix in the explorer

The patterns come from the synthetic cohort, shaped after the WHISH study. Scores for other people and devices are approximate.

One person's change mixes real change with day-to-day noise, so treat single results as a sketch. More valid days give a steadier picture. This page runs a simplified fit of the method so it can work in a browser. For research use, fit with fshapesTK as described on How it works.

Your privacy

Files are read by this page in your browser and are never sent anywhere. Closing the page discards them.
